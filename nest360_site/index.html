Data-driven tools to help hospitals and programs plan medical equipment needs for small and sick newborn care.

About NEST360

NEST360 is an alliance working to end preventable newborn deaths by improving access to essential technologies and quality care for small and sick newborns in health facilities. The alliance supports a multi-country neonatal care improvement programme across Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Nigeria, and Tanzania.

Device Quantity Estimation Tools

A core challenge for hospitals and programs is knowing how many units of a given medical device are actually needed to serve a hospital's admissions safely. This initiative builds simple, free calculators that estimate device quantity needs directly from empirical patient-level data, rather than generic assumptions.

The first tool covers CPAP (Continuous Positive Airway Pressure) devices. Additional device estimators are planned for future releases.
